Microcosm Arcade

Steer an amoeba, fire pulses, and survive incoming waves.

◈ Pulse
Press Space to aim at the nearest enemy or click and hold to aim. A hit does 25 damage.
↠ Dash
Use Shift or F while moving to burst clear of incoming damage. Use WASD, arrows, or the on-screen direction buttons to steer.
+ Mend
Swim over a green cross to restore up to 25 HP.
✦ Boost
Swim over an orange star for double pulse damage for 8 seconds.

Recognize enemies

Blue Drifters have 50 HP and hurt on contact. Orange Dart Ciliates have 75 HP. Move out of the red line before they charge.

Look inside the cells

The amoeba illustration shows a nucleus and a flexible cell membrane. The Dart Ciliate has bands of short cilia. Pulses, damage points, and power-ups are game rules.
