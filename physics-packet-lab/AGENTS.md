# Motion Lab project guidance

Read `maintainer/TEACHING_PREFERENCES.md` before adding or revising instructional material. Preserve the teacher’s preference for varied numbers, resulting answers, and realistic units across problems and practice variants.

Keep all physics calculations in consistent SI units. When a question uses other units, synchronize the displayed wording, answer input, conversion steps, packet answer key, and simulation.

Navigation between the packet and simulation must stay in the same tab and preserve the active variant and its saved work. Keep original and practice responses separate.
