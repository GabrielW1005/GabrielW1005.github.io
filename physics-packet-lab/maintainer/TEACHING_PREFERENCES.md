# Teaching material preferences

Gabriel requests varied values and realistic units in generated math and science materials.

- Avoid repeatedly choosing the same numerical answers, both within one problem type and across different topics.
- Vary whole numbers, decimals, fractions, signs where meaningful, and the scale of measurements while keeping arithmetic appropriate for the students.
- Use a practical variety of units that fits the situation (for example m/cm/km, s/min/h, m/s/km/h, kg/g, and N/kN). Do not switch units merely to make the wording complicated.
- Show necessary unit conversions clearly in worked examples and keep simulation calculations internally consistent.
- Practice variants must keep their exact measurements, answer units, explanations, and saved responses synchronized between the packet and simulation.
- Preserve published packet originals unless an edit to those originals is explicitly requested.

These preferences were explicitly requested on October 25, 2026 and apply to future updates to this project.
