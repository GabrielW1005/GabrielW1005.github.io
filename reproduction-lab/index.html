Why does variation matter?

Imagine a disease spreads through a population. How might genetic variation change which individuals survive?

Reveal a possible answer

Sexual reproduction can give offspring different combinations of alleles. If some combinations help individuals resist the disease, those individuals may be more likely to survive and reproduce. Asexual reproduction can be faster and can work without finding a mate. Mutation can introduce new alleles in either mode.
